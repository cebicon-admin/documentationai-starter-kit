## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

<Callout kind="info">

- A valid email address for account registration
- Browser support: Latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- Admin access for tenant configuration (if setting up for a team)

</Callout>

## Create Account and Log In

Follow these steps to create your OmniTruss account and access the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit `https://app.omnitruss.de/signup` and enter your email, password, and company details.
    
    Agree to the terms and click **Create Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from OmniTruss.
    
    Click the verification link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to `https://app.omnitruss.de/login`.
    
    Enter your credentials and click **Sign In**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Initial Tenant Setup

Tenants enable multi-client support. Configure your first tenant now.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Primary Tenant" icon="home">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Navigate to Tenants" icon="list">
        Click **Tenants** in the sidebar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add Tenant" icon="plus">
        Click **New Tenant** and fill in:
        
        - Name: `My First Tenant`
        - Domain: `tenant1.example.com`
        
        Save changes.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API Setup" icon="code">
    Generate an API key for integrations.
    
    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.example.com/v1/tenants/YOUR_TENANT_ID/api-keys \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{"name": "Dashboard Integration"}'
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Create Your First Self-Service Portal

Self-service portals let customers access docs, submit requests, and use apps without support tickets.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Portal Template" icon="layout">
    Go to **Self-Service** > **New Portal**.
    
    Choose **Customer Documentation** template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize Content" icon="edit-3">
    Add your branding:
    
    <CodeGroup tabs="JSON,CLI">
    ````json
    {
      "title": "Customer Portal",
      "logo": "https://your-logo.com/logo.png",
      "color": "#ed6c1a"
    }
    ````
    ````bash
    omnitruss portal create --template docs --title "Customer Portal" --color "#ed6c1a"
    ````
    </CodeGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish" icon="globe">
    Preview, then click **Publish**.
    
    Share the URL: `https://portal.example.com/tenant1`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Expandable title="Troubleshooting Common Issues" default-open="false">
  - **Login fails?** Clear browser cache or try incognito mode.
  - **Tenant not visible?** Refresh the dashboard or check permissions.
  - **Portal customization errors?** Validate JSON config with a linter.
</Expandable>
